Biswarup Biswas

Assistant Professor

Department of Mathematics, Mahindra University, Hyderabad

Specializing in hyperbolic conservation laws, WENO schemes, and entropy-stable high-order methods. Focused on robust, structure-preserving numerical techniques for compressible and astrophysical flows.

About Me

I am currently an Assistant Professor at Mahindra University, Hyderabad, where I work on computational methods for balance laws.

Before joining Mahindra University, I was a Post Doctoral Fellow at IIT Delhi, where I worked with Prof. Harish Kumar on the development of entropy stable methods.

I earned my PhD from SRM Institute of Science and Technology under the supervision of Prof. Ritesh Kumar Dubey. I completed my MSc from IIT Madras.

Current Focus: I am working on stable schemes for various astrophysical problems including simulations of relativistic flows and developing efficient numerical methods with theoretical guarantees.
