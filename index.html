§1 About Me

Current position, background, and research focus

I am currently an Assistant Professor at Mahindra University, Hyderabad, where I work on computational methods for balance laws, designing high-order schemes with provable stability guarantees. Before joining Mahindra University, I was a Post Doctoral Fellow at IIT Delhi, where I collaborated with Prof. Harish Kumar on the development of entropy stable methods for complex hyperbolic systems arising in plasma physics and relativistic flows. I earned my PhD from SRM Institute of Science and Technology under the supervision of Prof. Ritesh Kumar Dubey, working on nonlinearly stable high-order schemes for systems of hyperbolic conservation laws. I completed my MSc from IIT Madras.

Current Focus: I am working on stable schemes for astrophysical problems including simulations of relativistic flows and developing efficient numerical methods with theoretical guarantees — where mathematical rigor meets computational physics.

Entropy Stable Schemes DG Schemes WENO Schemes Structure-Preserving Methods Hyperbolic Conservation Laws Deep Learning Hamilton–Jacobi Equations Astrophysical Flows

§6 Research Projects

Funded research initiatives

Simulation of Relativistic Astrophysical Flow Problems
Development and implementation of modern efficient stable schemes for simulating relativistic astrophysical flows. This project focuses on creating provably stable numerical methods with applications to neutron star mergers, black hole accretion, and other extreme astrophysical phenomena where standard numerical techniques break down.
